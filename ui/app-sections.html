Add a library

A shared folder on this Synology, like /volume1/audiobooks (File Station → right-click the folder → Properties shows its location). Audiobooks go one folder per book, or Author → Book; podcasts one folder per show. A folder that isn't there is refused, never created.

Audiobookshelf's own app keeps your place in every book and podcast in sync with the web player and your other devices, and downloads for listening offline. Point it at the address below and sign in with your Audiobookshelf username and password.
